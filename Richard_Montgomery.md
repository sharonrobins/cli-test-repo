# Research Overview

I work mostly in the **classical N-body problem**. I also work in **differential geometry**, more specifically **sub-Riemannian geometry**, and a bit in **control theory**.

## Ongoing N-body projects

I have two ongoing N-body projects using help from AI (Claude):

1. **The free-fall 3-body problem**
2. **The 4-body problem in space**

Both involve building and understanding a **symbolic dynamics** for the problems.
