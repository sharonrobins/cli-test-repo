# Surya Raghavendran

## Research Interests

I'm a mathematical physicist using tools from derived geometry and representation
theory to elucidate aspects of supergravity and string theory. Much of my current
work concerns moduli problems associated to super-geometric and categorical data,
their linearizations, and their roles in BPS sectors of supergravity and string
theory. Such structures help rigorously formulate string dualities and illuminate
deformation-theoretic aspects of constructions in geometric representation theory.

## A Problem

Let $\Sigma$ be a proper curve, and let $\mathcal{C}$ be the 2-category
$2\mathrm{IndCoh}(\Sigma)$ of IndCoherent sheaves of categories on $\Sigma$. The problem is
to identify an object $\mathcal{F} \in 2\mathrm{IndCoh}(\Sigma)$ such that there is an
equivalence of formal moduli problems

$$\mathrm{ObjDef}_F \cong \mathrm{Op}_{SL_n}(\Sigma)^{\wedge}$$

between the one describing deformations of $\mathcal{F}$ and a formal neighborhood of a
point in the moduli of $SL_n$ opers. This corroborates a conjecture that the
nebulous superconformal field theories describing the low-energy dynamics of
M-theory's fivebranes are typified as moduli problems describing deformations
of objects in 2-categories.
