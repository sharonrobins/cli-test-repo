## Who am I? 

My name is Zhong Zhang. I am a fourth-year graduate student at the University of Chicago.

## My Mathematical interests:

I am interested in topology and geometry in general. I like to think about algebro-geometric objects in families. Specifically, I think a lot about moduli spaces and their underlying topological structures, and how this interacts with properties of the objects they parametrize. Recently, I have been thinking about representations of mapping class groups, congruence subgroups of braid groups, and branched covers.
