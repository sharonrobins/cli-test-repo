## Problem: Curves over finite fields
Rational points of curves over finite satisfy the so called Weil bound. 
Then a natural question is to find curves that satisfy this bound.
But it is known that this bound is not always sharp. So a natural question is to decide when this bound is sharp and if it is not what arguments one can give to lower the bound and possible to find a "sharp" curve. As we expect "proofs", since we might not find curves satisfying the Weil bound, this problem could have a Lean (auto)formalization aspect.
